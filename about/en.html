About Biblia Sin Prisas

Biblia Sin Prisas is a free platform for reading and comparing 14 Bible editions in six languages: five in Spanish, five in English, and one each in Russian, Portuguese, French and German.

Reading and comparison

Choose a book, chapter and verse. Compare up to four translations side by side, with corresponding verses aligned in the same row.

Highlight words or passages. Highlights and preferences are saved in this browser. YouTube recommendations appear when a video matches the passage you are reading.

The reader adapts to desktops, tablets and phones. It requires no account and carries no third-party advertising. The reading controls require JavaScript.
